# 💖 Maria’s Digital Sanctuary (Bebo) — Romantic Maximalism & Y2K Digital Scrapbook

> An ultra-expressive, high-energy digital scrapbook featuring 144 curated memories, tactile polaroids, washi tape accents, neo-brutalist typography, retro cassette audio player, and heartfelt storytelling chapters for Maria (Bebo).

---

## 🌟 Key Highlights

1. **Design System & Visual Tokens**:
   - Base textured grain/parchment (`#FFFDF5`) with subtle grid / risograph halftone dot pattern.
   - Core Brand Accents: Hot Cherry (`#FF1268`), Electric Tangerine (`#FF5E00`), Sunshine Gold (`#FFB800`), Neon Acid Lime (`#BBF734`), and Midnight Violet (`#190934`).
   - Neo-brutalist offset borders (`3px solid #190934`) with hard drop shadows (`6px 6px 0px #190934`).
2. **Typography System**:
   - Massive editorial headlines (*Syne* / *Playfair Display* via fluid `clamp(3rem, 7.5vw, 7.25rem)`).
   - Monospaced / bold geometric badges (*Space Grotesk* / *JetBrains Mono*) in ALL-CAPS with pill borders.
   - Fluid cursive handwriting accents (*Caveat* / *Reenie Beanie*).
3. **Sticker-Bombed Y2K Cyber Lockscreen**:
   - Passcode key: `123987` (iPad PIN).
   - Chunky tactile keypad buttons with physical depression depth and sticker badges.
4. **Editorial Cover Hero with Dual Infinite Marquees**:
   - Dual animated infinite marquee ribbons moving in opposite directions (`marquee-band`).
   - Eclectic relationship counter dashboard with candy-colored cards and washi tape clips.
5. **Storytelling Narrative Chapters**:
   - Overlapping polaroid scrapbook layout with marker captions and sticker stamps (*"FAVORITE MEMORY ★"*, *"100% CUTIE"*).
6. **144-Item Cluttered Polaroid Wall & Cinema Lightbox**:
   - Organized-chaos masonry collage of polaroid frames, film sprockets, barcodes, and postage marks with 3D hover pop.
   - Inline video auto-play on viewport entry (`IntersectionObserver`).
7. **Retro Cassette / Mini-Disc Audio Player**:
   - Spinning tape reels, real-time equalizer visualizer, and tactile controls playing `1.mp3`.
8. **Interactive Love Letter & Grand Finale**:
   - Crimson envelope with 3D wax seal button opening to authentic lined notebook stationery paper with margin line and typewriter cursive font.
   - Grand Climax button with confetti explosions and screen flash.
9. **Secret Cyber-Vault**:
   - Passcode key: `210604` (Configurable in `config.js`; inspection blocked for privacy).
